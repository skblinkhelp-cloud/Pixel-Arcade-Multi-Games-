Privacy Policy for MultiGame Arcade

Effective Date: October 8, 2026

SK Software ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application MultiGame Arcade (Package Name: com.sk.multigamearcade).

1. Information Collection and Use

MultiGame Arcade does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII).

  • Local Data Storage: All game progress, high scores, favorite games, and audio/vibration settings are saved locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device.
  • No User Registration: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide any personal information to play games in MultiGame Arcade.

2. Permissions Used

The application requests minimal standard Android device permissions necessary for gameplay features:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used solely to provide optional haptic feedback during arcade game interactions.

3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

MultiGame Arcade does not use third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or targeted advertising services that collect user data.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application is designed for general audiences of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or applicable age in your jurisdiction).

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
